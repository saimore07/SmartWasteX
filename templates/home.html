PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Institutional Sustainability

SmartWasteX connects student waste sorting habits directly with facilities operations and certified recycling partners.

AI Waste Classification

Computer vision heuristics and edge feature extraction analyze items in real time, accurately segregating Biodegradable, Dry Recyclable, and Hazardous E-Waste streams with instant handling advice.

Instant Confidence Score
Smart Bin Telemetry

Continuous monitoring of bin fill levels, load weights, ambient temperatures, and node battery status. Predictive heuristics estimate remaining hours until critical capacity is reached.

Ultrasonic & Load Cells
Circular E-Waste Tracking

Institutional IT hardware is tagged with dynamic Digital Passport QR codes. Tracks custody from Registration and Decommissioning to Collection and certified R2 Recycler dispatch.

Public QR Verification
Collection Dispatch System

Automatic collection requests trigger when receptacles reach Warning (70%), High (85%), or Overflow (100%) thresholds. Facilities logistics teams can assign, update, and resolve tickets.

Dynamic Prioritization
Green Credits & Leaderboard

Users earn verified green credits for proper segregation and e-waste handovers. A campus-wide leaderboard motivates student bodies and academic departments toward zero-waste goals.

Campus Eco Champions
Sustainability Command Center

Centralized officer dashboard featuring interactive GIS campus map overlays, multi-stream segregation analytics, bin health monitoring, and institutional carbon offset calculations.
